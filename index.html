Line Hunter

Scans every market for one game across sportsbooks, finds where the books priced something wrong, and builds the best bets at three stake levels.

Paste from as many sportsbooks as you can. With a single book there's nothing to compare against, so almost nothing shows as an edge. Markets: spread, moneyline, total, team_total, alt_spread, alt_total, and player stats (for example pass_yds, rec_yds, points, pra, alt_points). Odds can be American (-110) or decimal (1.91).
How the model finds book mistakes

No model wins every bet. The edge shows up over hundreds of bets. Only stake money you can afford to lose. Problem gambling help: 1-800-GAMBLER.
